Expert Suggestions: Lung Cancer Awareness

Treatments

  • Surgery: Removal of tumors and affected lung tissues.
  • Radiation Therapy: High-energy rays to kill cancer cells.
  • Chemotherapy: Drug treatment to shrink or eliminate cancer.
  • Targeted Therapy: Attacks specific abnormalities in cancer cells.
  • Immunotherapy: Boosts the body's immune system to fight cancer.

How to Avoid Lung Cancer

  • Avoid smoking (both active and passive).
  • Maintain indoor air quality, avoid radon and pollutants.
  • Wear masks if working in chemical or construction zones.
  • Eat a diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and antioxidants.
  • Exercise regularly and stay physically active.

Major Causes of Lung Cancer

  • Smoking (primary cause — up to 85%).
  • Exposure to asbestos, radon gas, and industrial pollutants.
  • Family history of lung cancer.
  • Prolonged exposure to air pollution.
  • Radiation therapy to the chest (e.g., for other cancers).
